Privacy Policy

This policy explains what MarginQuote handles, where data stays, and what happens during purchase and export.

The short version

MarginQuote requires no account, shows no ads, performs no cross-app tracking, and includes no third-party analytics SDK. Client, quote, cost, profit, and business profile data are not sent to a developer server.

Data stored on the device

The app stores business profile details, client names, quote items, currencies, prices, costs, fees, discounts, margin targets, notes, and quote status on the device so you can edit quotes and create files.

MarginQuote has no account or cross-device sync. Local app data may be included in Apple device backups according to system settings. Deleting the app deletes its data from the current device.

PDF and CSV export

A file leaves the app only when you create it and choose a destination in the system share sheet. The selected service then handles it under its own privacy policy. Client PDFs exclude internal cost and profit data. Pro CSV exports contain saved quote details.

Apple In-App Purchase

Apple App Store and StoreKit handle purchase, restore, refund, and transaction verification. MarginQuote unlocks Pro only for an Apple-verified, non-revoked transaction. It has no developer receipt server and receives no payment-card information.

Permissions, network, and website logs

The app requests no camera, photo, microphone, contact, or location permission. Core quote calculation and file generation work locally. Purchase, restore, and opening this website require a network connection.

The hosting service may process standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure this website. The site has no advertising tracker and does not build user profiles.

Updates and contact

If the app's actual data behavior changes, this page will be updated before the corresponding release. Questions can be sent through the support page.
