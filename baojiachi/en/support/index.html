Common questions

What is the minimum safe quote?

It is the minimum client total that can cover current costs and platform fees while reaching your target margin. It is a business calculation, not tax, legal, or pricing advice.

Why does the app warn that a quote loses money?

The client total does not cover item costs, additional costs, and the platform fee. MarginQuote shows the shortfall and never changes your input.

Can a client see my internal costs?

No. Client PDFs show client-facing items, quantities, prices, totals, validity, and public terms. They do not show internal costs, profit, or target margin.

How do currencies work?

Choose the default currency for new quotes in Settings. Every saved quote keeps its own currency and official decimal precision.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the Pro page from Settings and tap Restore Purchase. Only an Apple-verified, non-revoked purchase unlocks Pro.

Does MarginQuote create tax invoices or collect payment?

No. It creates ordinary business quotes and does not provide tax invoices, tax advice, contract signing, or online payment.
